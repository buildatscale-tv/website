Our Story

Three Decades of
Artisanal Excellence

Founded in 1987 by master craftsman Thomas Hartley, Hartwood Custom Cabinets has been transforming homes across the region with bespoke cabinetry that blends timeless artistry with modern functionality.

Every piece that leaves our workshop carries the mark of meticulous attention to detail — from hand-selected hardwoods to precision joinery that stands the test of time. We believe that true craftsmanship is invisible: the drawers glide silently, the doors align perfectly, and the finish feels like silk under your fingertips.

Our team of skilled artisans combines traditional woodworking techniques with contemporary design sensibilities, creating pieces that are as beautiful as they are functional. Whether it's a complete kitchen renovation or a single custom vanity, we approach every project with the same dedication to excellence.
